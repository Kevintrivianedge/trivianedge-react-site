# Journalist-quote platforms — ready to respond

These are the legitimate replacement for HARO (Cision retired the HARO brand
into Connectively, which then shut down in late 2024). Unlike directory
listings, these are query-driven: you don't submit once, you check in and
respond when a live question matches what TrivianEdge actually knows about.
The link only happens if a real journalist genuinely uses your quote — that's
the part that can't be automated, and shouldn't be.

## Where to watch

1. **Featured** (featured.com) — acquired the HARO brand in 2025, now runs
   three daily email digests of live journalist questions. Free tier exists;
   this is the highest-volume source.
2. **Qwoted** (qwoted.com) — smaller, more vetted, skews toward
   higher-authority outlets. Free tier available.
3. **SourceBottle** (sourcebottle.com) — free, journalist-facing, lower
   volume but zero cost to monitor.
4. **MentionMatch** (formerly Help a B2B Writer, now run by the Superpath
   content-marketing community) — B2B-specific, worth a look given
   TrivianEdge's audience, though it's community/Slack-based rather than a
   standalone feed, so search "MentionMatch Superpath" to find current access.

All four email or feed you the questions — no login automation needed to see
what's available. The move each time: skim for a match against the six
angles below, paste + personalize, send from a real inbox.

## Six angles TrivianEdge can genuinely speak to

Each of these maps to something real in the business, not a generic
"thought leadership" filler take.

1. **Why the outsourcing playbook built pre-AI/pre-remote-work is breaking.**
   Kevin's founding thesis: 20+ years watching companies fail not because
   offshore talent doesn't work, but because the old model (fixed hours
   billed, no AI-assisted delivery) doesn't match how founders now expect
   partners to move.

2. **What actually breaks when a company scales an offshore team without
   an employer-of-record partner.** Concrete failure modes: broken hiring
   pipelines, no operational ownership, teams that are staffed but not
   managed. Grounded in the Cargo Login and Keynotive case studies.

3. **The real cost difference between local hiring and offshore staffing
   for BPO/RPO roles.** TrivianEdge has an actual number to cite (up to 40%
   cost savings) and a live calculator (trivianedge.com/savings-calculator)
   a journalist can be pointed to for a working example, not just a claim.

4. **How AI is changing recruitment process outsourcing (RPO)
   specifically** — sourcing/screening augmentation with a human recruiter
   still reviewing every shortlist, as opposed to full automation. Directly
   maps to TrivianEdge's AI-Powered Recruitment service line.

5. **What it actually takes for a founder to expand into a new region
   (e.g., GCC/Middle East) without a local entity.** Grounded in the
   Hub-Flx case study — medtech/ERP/data-migration rollout support.

6. **RAG vs. fine-tuning for companies evaluating AI development
   partners** — a genuinely technical angle (already a full blog post at
   trivianedge.com/blog/rag-vs-fine-tuning) for outlets covering AI
   implementation rather than pure business-ops angles.

## Logging

Every quote that actually gets published goes in `tracker.csv` with the
outlet, live URL, and date — that's the actual backlink, and it's worth
checking a few weeks after sending in case a piece runs late.
