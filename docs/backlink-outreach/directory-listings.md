# Directory listings — ready to submit

Six real, relevant directories for a Toronto-based BPO/RPO/software
outsourcing company. Each gets you a company profile page with a follow or
nofollow link back to trivianedge.com — legitimate because it's your own
verified business profile, the same category as a G2 or Google Business
listing, not a manufactured link.

For each: go to the domain below, look for "Get Listed" / "For Vendors" /
"Claim your profile" (usually in the footer or top nav — exact URLs move
around, so this points you to the real domain rather than a guessed deep
link that might 404). Paste the relevant profile copy when their form asks
for a company description.

---

## 1. Clutch (clutch.co)
The highest-trust option — verified, interview-based client reviews. Worth
the effort even though it's the slowest to complete (they call your listed
clients to verify the case studies).

**Profile copy (short, ~150 char):**
> Toronto-based BPO, RPO, and bespoke software development company. TrivianEdge deploys offshore teams in 30 days, up to 40% cost savings across 6 time zones.

**Profile copy (long):**
> TrivianEdge is a Toronto, Ontario-based BPO, RPO, and bespoke software development company. We act as employer of record across six global talent hubs — the Philippines, Sri Lanka, Vietnam, Turkey, South Africa, and Costa Rica — so clients deploy offshore teams in as little as 30 days without setting up a foreign entity themselves. Services span Business Process Outsourcing, Recruitment Process Outsourcing, bespoke software development (custom development, cloud/DevOps, QA, IT outsourcing), and AI development. Founded 2023.

**Case studies to reference when they ask for verifiable client work:**
Cargo Login (logistics ops coverage model), Keynotive (parallel offshore outbound teams), Hub-Flx (GCC/UAE market expansion support).

---

## 2. GoodFirms (goodfirms.co)
Note the TLD is `.co`, not `.com`. Less strict verification than Clutch,
faster to get listed, ranks well for IT/BPO category searches in its own
right.

**Profile copy:** same short/long copy as Clutch above.

**Category to select:** BPO Services / Business Process Outsourcing (they
also have IT Outsourcing and Software Development categories worth a
secondary listing if the form allows more than one).

---

## 3. Sortlist (sortlist.com)
Buyer-matchmaking model — companies post a brief, agencies respond. Getting
listed here also puts you in their inbound-match pool, not just a static
profile.

**Profile copy:** same short copy as above.

**Specialization tags to select:** Business Process Outsourcing, Staff
Augmentation, Custom Software Development.

---

## 4. Outsource Accelerator (outsourceaccelerator.com)
Free, outsourcing-specific directory of 2,300+ firms with an engaged
industry audience (their own site says 25,000+ professionals use their
resources). Directly on-topic for TrivianEdge's exact category — worth
prioritizing over generic B2B directories.

**Profile copy:** long copy from Clutch above, since this audience already
knows outsourcing terminology.

---

## 5. Outsourcing Digest (outsourcingdigest.com)
Serves both BPO buyers and providers; smaller than Outsource Accelerator but
cheap to add given the profile copy is already written.

**Profile copy:** short copy from Clutch above.

---

## 6. DesignRush (designrush.com)
Broader agency directory with a dedicated BPO category page — good for
generalist buyer traffic beyond the outsourcing-specific crowd above.

**Profile copy:** short copy from Clutch above.

**Category:** Call Centers → BPO / Sales Outsourcing.

---

## After submitting

Log each one in `tracker.csv`: date submitted, status (pending review /
live / rejected), and the live profile URL once it's up — useful both for
your own record and because a couple of these (Clutch, GoodFirms) are worth
linking to from the TrivianEdge `/trust` or `/proof` page once verified,
which is a legitimate two-way trust signal.
